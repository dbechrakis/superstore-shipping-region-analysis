Metric definitions & interpretation

Profit margin is calculated from aggregate profit and sales, never by averaging row-level percentages. Order lines are distinct transaction rows. The source contains 9,994 lines and 5,009 unique orders; unique orders are not summed across product groups.

Observed margin differences motivate investigation of product mix, pricing and discounting. They do not establish causal effects or the profit uplift of a proposed intervention. Dispatch time and delivery time are different metrics; this profitability view does not infer delivery performance.
